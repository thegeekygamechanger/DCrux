## DCrux 1.4.7

**Phone:** download `DCrux-1.4.7.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update).
**Computer:** Windows `DCrux-1.4.7-windows-x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`), Linux `DCrux-1.4.7-linux-x64.deb` or `DCrux-1.4.7-linux-x64.tar.gz` (unpack and run `DCrux/bin/DCrux`).

### New
- **Subject-wise tests, for Prelims and Mains.** In Tests, pick a subject above the Generate button: Polity & Governance, History & Culture, Geography, Economy, Environment & Ecology, Science & Technology, International Relations, Society & Social Justice, Internal Security, and (for Mains) Ethics. "All subjects" is the test on the day's news, as before.
- A subject test takes its questions from this week's news where it touches the subject, and from the subject's standard syllabus for the rest. Every question says which it is: the news item it came from, or "Standard syllabus".

### Since 1.4.5 on a computer
- Opens about twice as fast from the second start, uses about 100 MB less memory, and starts with a small DCrux card and a fade-in instead of a blank window.

### Good to know
- Syllabus questions are written by Crux AI from settled, textbook-level facts. They are a revision aid; check anything that surprises you against your own books.
- The Windows build is made and started by an automated check on GitHub; it has not yet been used by hand.
