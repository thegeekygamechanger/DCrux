## DCrux 1.3.2

**Install:** download `DCrux-1.3.2.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update). No account and no API key needed.

### New
- **Mains answer evaluation.** Tests now has two sides, Prelims and Mains. In Mains, pick your exam, day, difficulty and marks, write each answer on paper against the clock, then photograph the pages or upload a scan (photos or PDF). The AI reads the handwriting and marks it the way a mentor would: marks on five fixed criteria, what to fix first, mistakes quoted from your own answer, what a top answer adds, a topper's outline, exam-hall tips and time advice against the exam's pace. Each answer comes back as a marked copy: page by page and section by section, your own words with a tick, half-tick or cross and a short remark beside each. You can always open "What the AI read from your pages" to check the reading.
- **Ask Crux AI, wherever you are reading.** The assistant is now called Crux AI. Besides Papers, it is on every video's Summary and Detailed notes and in read mode, including full screen. Ask a question or a doubt and the answer comes from the video or article first; when that does not cover it, Crux AI searches the web and lists the pages it used; when it still cannot confirm an answer, it says so. Answers can carry a diagram or a picture. Only answers are shown, not the working.
- **History page** (Settings → History): every question you asked, with when and on which video or article, and every test, with filters and pages. Open any entry to read the answer again or jump back to the source.
- **Retake, delete, filter and sort in test history.** Every entry is labelled Prelims or Mains. Filter by type, sort by date or score, retake a test to beat your own score (the history keeps the better attempt), or delete a result.
- **A relevance score on every question**, Prelims and Mains, out of 100 for the exam you chose.
- **A celebration** when you score well.
- **Your day on the home screen.** The date and time over a small animated landscape that follows the hour, the weather and the temperature of your city (sun, moon and stars, cloud, rain, snow, fog, storms), and looks a little different each time. Tap it for the details and for weather alerts (heat, heavy rain, storms, fog, bad air). Location is optional: allow it or type your city.
- **Pull down to sync.** The refresh button is gone; drag the Crux screen down instead. A short tour shows this the first time.
- **Search slides up** over whatever screen you are on.
- **One home card.** The daily quotes now sit on the date and weather card instead of a separate carousel.
- **About** now has the app details, how the AI works, privacy, terms and licences, each in its own slide-up sheet.

### Changed
- A calmer colour palette: indigo and slate, with no pink or red on buttons and selected controls.
- The sync line shows only while syncing, says "Synced", and then gets out of the way.
- Leaving a test, a Mains attempt or full-screen reading part-way asks you to finish first.
- The free AI service now allows far more requests per phone per day, so normal use does not hit a limit.

### Good to know
- A Mains attempt is saved as you go. If your phone closes the app to open the camera, the attempt and the photo are still there when you come back.
- Page photos are deleted from the phone once the evaluation is done. The AI's reading and feedback are stored encrypted with your result.
- Crux AI is for exam preparation: it does not write code. No AI is always right; check important facts against the sources it shows.
- Mains marks are a strict AI estimate to learn from, not an official score. If the reading of your page is wrong, retake the photo in good light and evaluate again.
- Weather alerts are worked out from forecast data (Open-Meteo) and are not official warnings.
