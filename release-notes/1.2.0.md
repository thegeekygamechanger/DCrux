## DCrux 1.2.0

**Install:** download `DCrux-1.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. No account and no API key needed.

### New
- **Updates inside the app.** DCrux now checks GitHub for a newer version once a day (or when you ask in Settings → App update), downloads it and hands it to Android to install. No APK file is left on your phone.
- **Full screen in read mode.** A new button gives the page the whole display; tap the corner button or press back to leave.
- Cleaner home header: just the name.

### From 1.1.0
- Read mode opens video notes as a book: a cover with the contents, then one page per topic, turned like paper.
- Summaries are written from what is said in the video, with photos and diagrams.
- Works out of the box through the DCrux service, with a fair-use daily limit per phone.

AI-written text can be wrong. Check important facts against the original video or article.
