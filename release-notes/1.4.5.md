## DCrux 1.4.5

**Phone:** download `DCrux-1.4.5.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update).
**Computer:** Windows `DCrux-1.4.5-windows-x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`), Linux `DCrux-1.4.5-linux-x64.deb` or `DCrux-1.4.5-linux-x64.tar.gz` (unpack and run `DCrux/bin/DCrux`).

### New
- **Send feedback.** Settings → Send feedback opens a short form: say whether it is a problem or an idea, write your message, and leave your email if you would like a reply (usually within a few hours).

### Fixed
- **Search finds what you mean.** Words can now be typed in any order, a date such as `14 nov` or `14/11` is understood, the channel's name counts, and one word that is not in the title no longer hides the result. Searching "prashant sir 14 nov current affairs" now brings up that day's video.
- **Tests on a computer.** The Prelims/Mains switch, your score and the new-test card now line up edge to edge and at the same height in a wide window.
- **Smoother tabs on a computer.** The highlight in the side rail glides to the tab you choose and the page eases in after it, as on the phone.

### Since 1.4.1
- Settings redesigned into groups whose rows slide their controls up from the bottom.
- Pair devices by typing a 4-character code (or scanning the QR code). The code works once and is not the key to your data.
- The sync service keeps nothing on disk; it only passes your encrypted data between your devices.
- A new monochrome app icon.

### Good to know
- A feedback message is passed on to the developer by email with the address you chose to give, the app version, and whether you wrote from a phone or a computer. Nothing else is sent with it.
- The Windows build is made and started by an automated check on GitHub; it has not yet been used by hand.
