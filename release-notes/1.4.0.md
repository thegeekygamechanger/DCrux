## DCrux 1.4.0

**Phone:** download `DCrux-1.4.0.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update).
**Computer:** `DCrux-1.4.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` for any Linux (unpack and run `DCrux/bin/DCrux`). A Windows installer (`.msi`) and a `.deb` can be built by the GitHub workflow in this repository; they are added here once that build has been run.

### New
- **DCrux for Windows and Linux.** The same app, screen for screen: Crux, Papers, Ask Crux AI, Tests (Prelims and Mains), History, Mind Flow, Settings. It is built from the same code as the phone app, so the two stay the same.
- **Sync across your devices, with no account.** In Settings → Sync across devices, turn it on and you get a 25-character code; enter it on your other device. Channels, video notes, questions and answers, test results and study settings are then the same everywhere. Everything is encrypted on your device with a key made from the code before it is uploaded, so the service stores data it cannot read. PDFs and page photos are never uploaded. You can erase the stored copy at any time, which also switches sync off on your other devices.

- **Pair by QR code.** The device with sync on shows a QR code; on the phone choose "Scan the QR code on another device" and photograph it. The typed code still works.
- **Landscape on a computer.** A wide window gets a navigation rail at the side, lists that flow into columns, and newspaper pages set in three columns like a broadsheet. Made narrow, the window turns into the phone layout.

### What differs on a computer
- **Video notes are written on the phone**, where the app can listen to the video; they arrive on the computer through sync. On the computer a video opens on YouTube in your browser.
- **Articles open in your own browser**; "Read it here" brings the text into read mode.
- **Mains answers come from files**: photos or a PDF of your pages (from a scanner, or sent from your phone). There is no camera button.
- **Sync button instead of pulling down**; **Esc** goes back; page numbers stay visible in full-screen reading.
- **Not on the computer yet:** the result PDF, and unlocking with a fingerprint (the PIN lock works).
- The computer build keeps its encryption key in a file only your own user account can read; a phone keeps it in the secure hardware. The Windows build was made and started by an automated check, but has not been used by hand yet: tell us what you find.

### Changed (phone)
- The app was reorganised inside so that the phone and the computer share their screens. Nothing should look or behave differently; if something does, that is a bug.
