## DCrux 1.3.3

**Install:** download `DCrux-1.3.3.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update).

### New
- **A Stop button wherever Crux AI is writing:** video summaries and detailed notes, Ask Crux AI, the search overview, test and Mains question setting, Mains evaluation, and the sync's relevance check. In Ask Crux AI, what had been written before you stopped is kept and marked as unfinished.
- **You are asked before a video summary is written.** Opening a video no longer starts it by itself: choose Continue or Not now.

### Changed
- **Video notes read like notes, not like a transcript.** They are organised by concept, written plainly, and state the content directly. After writing, every line is checked a second time against the video's captions, and anything the captions do not support is corrected or removed. Where a figure is unclear in the video, the notes say so instead of guessing.
- **Ask Crux AI answers much faster.** It answers from the video or article first and brings in web search only when that does not hold the answer. On a full lecture, the first words now arrive in about 2 seconds instead of about 18 (measured), with the same model.

### Good to know
- Auto-generated captions can mishear names and numbers. The second check removes what the captions do not support, but it cannot hear the video better than the captions do. Check important figures against the video or a standard source.
