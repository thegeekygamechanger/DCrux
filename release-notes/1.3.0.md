## DCrux 1.3.0

**Install:** download `DCrux-1.3.0.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update). No account and no API key needed.

### New
- **Ask AI on every article.** In Papers, tap "Ask AI" on any headline to ask about it. Answers come from the article itself, and every question you ask stays saved with that article.
- **Sync that keeps working.** When YouTube's feed for a channel is down, DCrux now reads the channel's own pages instead of reporting an error.
- **Scores out of 100.** Exam relevance is now one clear score out of 100, built the same way for videos and headlines.
- **Video page scrolls as one.** The player moves up with the page instead of staying pinned.
- **Smaller bottom bar** and a **tidier Settings page**: one short list of sections that open on tap.

AI-written text can be wrong. Check important facts against the original video or article.
