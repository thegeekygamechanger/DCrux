## DCrux 1.3.4

**Install:** download `DCrux-1.3.4.apk` below, or let the app update itself (Settings → App update).

### Fixed
- **Air quality now uses India's own AQI scale.** The app was showing the US index worked out from a 24-hour average of modelled data, which read far too low on a bad evening (160 for Delhi when monitors there were reading 200 to 330). It now shows India's national AQI from the particle levels at the moment, with the bands used in India (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very poor, Severe), and the alerts follow those bands.
- The weather sheet says where the number comes from. Where the DCrux service has official CPCB monitor data for your city, it shows the city's AQI from those monitors, the worst station and the day's highest reading. Otherwise it is marked as an estimate.
